# Exora content catalog

All rows describe planned content. Names and exact recipes can change during design and playtesting.

| Age | Manufacturing and infrastructure | Equipment and vehicles | Overworld purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workshop | Alloy furnace, press, plates, gears, and workshop bench | Survey tools, mechanical tools, and repair kits | Survey ruins and restore early equipment |
| Electrical | Basic generator, processing machine, energy storage, cables, and conveyors | Powered tools and portable scanner | Restart a survey station and find resource deposits |
| Mechanized | Precision assembler, programmable routing, robot dock, and vehicle service bay | Utility robots, tracked utility vehicle, tank, and fictional conventional armaments | Recover industrial relays and handle heavier hostile constructs |
| Aerospace | Airframe assembly rig, hangar, flight instruments, and field supply station | Propeller fighter plane, fighter jet, helicopter, and cargo modules | Reach remote Overworld sites and escort field expeditions |
| Atomic | Fictional nuclear generator, containment multiblock, coolant loop, and shielded logistics | Protective powered suit, remote service robot, and heavy vehicle modules | Investigate a contaminated research complex and contain an original hazard |
| Fusion | Fusion multiblock, exotic material processor, and advanced equipment stations | Modular powersuits, pilotable mechs, high-capacity robots, and sci-fi weapons | Challenge the Overworld boss chain and operate in extreme environments |
| Frontier | Orbital fabrication modules, expedition infrastructure, and exotic power systems | Expedition craft and traversal modules | Support future space travel, planets, and dimensions |

Each equipment or vehicle family should have a research prerequisite, a manufacturing station, defined upgrade slots where relevant, operation costs, and a maintenance or recovery path. Each major system should unlock at least one factory or field capability.
