# Exora content catalog

The [master design](docs/MASTER_DESIGN.md#confirmed-end-goals) confirms the technology families below. The unlocks, fabrication and research objectives, locations, and names are proposed. The objectives use the tier numbers in the [progression spec](docs/PROGRESSION.md).

| Confirmed family and content | Proposed unlock | What the player achieves to access or use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fictional weapons, armored vehicles, and tanks | [3.2 Surface Industry](docs/PROGRESSION.md#3-mechanized) | Precision frames and drives enable the service bay. Commission a surface carrier before expanding to armor or optional fictional equipment modules. |
| Fighter planes and helicopters | [4.1 Flight](docs/PROGRESSION.md#4-aerospace) | Assemble and service an airframe at a hangar. Propeller fighters, helicopters, and cargo aircraft use common controls and maintenance. |
| Fighter jets and futuristic expedition equipment | [4.2 Expedition Systems](docs/PROGRESSION.md#4-aerospace) | Establish a serviceable flight loop, then use the advanced airframe rig and an expedition depot. |
| Fictional nukes, missiles, and bombs | [5.2 Shielded Power](docs/PROGRESSION.md#5-atomic) | Unlock optional sandbox equipment after the shielded power demonstration. Server permissions and designated encounter rules govern access and effects. |
| Energy blades, energy shields, and pilotable mechs | [5.2 Shielded Power](docs/PROGRESSION.md#5-atomic) and [6.2 Adaptive Systems](docs/PROGRESSION.md#6-fusion) | Shielded power opens early shields. High-energy fabrication and a service station open advanced shields, blades, and mech frames. |
| Exoskeletons and jetpacks | [3.2 Surface Industry](docs/PROGRESSION.md#3-mechanized) and [4.2 Expedition Systems](docs/PROGRESSION.md#4-aerospace) | Use precision mechanical parts for an exoskeleton. Flight infrastructure and compact energy storage support a jetpack. |
| Atomic-themed armor, modular powersuits, nanotech suits, and quantum armor | [5.1 Containment](docs/PROGRESSION.md#5-atomic), [6.2 Adaptive Systems](docs/PROGRESSION.md#6-fusion), [7.2 Planetary Logistics](docs/PROGRESSION.md#7-orbital-and-planetary), and [8.1 Field Control](docs/PROGRESSION.md#8-cosmic-engineering) | Progress from a containment suit to a serviceable modular platform, then add nanotech and quantum modules for off-world and field environments. |
| Cars, trucks, trains, and ships | [3.2 Surface Industry](docs/PROGRESSION.md#3-mechanized) | Manufacture a carrier at the vehicle bay and prove delivery across a road, rail, or water route. |
| Planes, submarines, hovercraft, and maglev | [4.1 Flight and 4.2 Expedition Systems](docs/PROGRESSION.md#4-aerospace) | Hangar operations enable planes. The pressure workshop, range relays, and depot enable subsea, hover, and maglev transport. |
| Drones, androids, robotic companions, artificial intelligence, and automated construction | [3.2 Surface Industry](docs/PROGRESSION.md#3-mechanized) and [6.2 Adaptive Systems](docs/PROGRESSION.md#6-fusion) | Dock and maintain drones, then use industrial controls, android assembly, and a construction controller to assign more capable jobs. |
| Computers, servers, digital storage, and industrial control networks | [3.3 Industrial Control](docs/PROGRESSION.md#3-mechanized) | Connect local storage and control to an operating line or service route. Use the network to route jobs and surface faults. |
| Supercomputers and quantum computers | [6.1 High-Energy Industry](docs/PROGRESSION.md#6-fusion) and [8.1 Field Control](docs/PROGRESSION.md#8-cosmic-engineering) | High-energy industry supports research and scheduling workloads. Orbital samples and field fabrication enable quantum control workloads. |
| Chemistry and material sciences | [2.2 Production Line](docs/PROGRESSION.md#2-electrical) and [3.1 Precision](docs/PROGRESSION.md#3-mechanized) | Run a simple processing line, then compare material properties at a lab to unlock better frames and specialty stock. |
| Atomic sciences and particle physics | [5.1 Containment](docs/PROGRESSION.md#5-atomic) and [6.1 High-Energy Industry](docs/PROGRESSION.md#6-fusion) | Verify fictional containment, then record field data at a high-energy research installation. |
| Biotechnology, cybernetics, and nanotechnology | [6.2 Adaptive Systems](docs/PROGRESSION.md#6-fusion) and [7.2 Planetary Logistics](docs/PROGRESSION.md#7-orbital-and-planetary) | Use fictional lab simulations to improve recovery, habitats, suits, and robots. Refine off-world stock for nanotech modules. |
| Rockets, satellites, and space stations | [7.1 Low Orbit](docs/PROGRESSION.md#7-orbital-and-planetary) | Certify Overworld return and rescue readiness, then commission a launch facility and make a supply trip to orbit and back. |
| Spaceships, asteroid mining, planetary colonies, and terraforming | [7.2 Planetary Logistics and 7.3 Habitats](docs/PROGRESSION.md#7-orbital-and-planetary) | Use a shipyard and cargo relays to ship off-world materials. Keep a colony supplied and operate in-game environmental controls in designated habitat zones. |
| Teleportation, gravity manipulation, matter replication, and holograms | [8.1 Field Control](docs/PROGRESSION.md#8-cosmic-engineering) | Use analyzed anomalies and a quantum-controlled station to demonstrate limited local transport, field effects, displays, and recipe-bound production. |
| Dimensional travel, artificial wormholes, and Dyson spheres | [8.2 Dimensional Industry and 8.3 Stellar Megaprojects](docs/PROGRESSION.md#8-cosmic-engineering) | Establish a reversible route, then use orbital assembly and sustained logistics to commission a working Dyson sphere sector. |
| Automated agriculture, medical chambers, environmental purification, advanced construction, and industrial infrastructure | [2.2 Production Line](docs/PROGRESSION.md#2-electrical), [5.3 Remediation](docs/PROGRESSION.md#5-atomic), and [6.2 Adaptive Systems](docs/PROGRESSION.md#6-fusion) | Keep a powered farm and purifier running, recover at a medical chamber, and supply the controlled construction of a working facility. These systems later support planetary habitats. |

For any equipment or vehicle family, define its research proof, fabrication or service station, operating costs, relevant upgrade slots, and a recovery path. Assign each machine or module at least one factory, field, or habitat job before adding variants. Use the same service and supply patterns across related families.
