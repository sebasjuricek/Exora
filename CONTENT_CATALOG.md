# Exora content catalog

This is the player-facing acquisition plan. Each row gives the first intended access, how the player gets a usable version, and the job it should do. Tier numbers link to the [progression spec](docs/PROGRESSION.md). A tier listed by name starts when the previous proof is registered, unless the row calls for an additional demonstration. The final column identifies a concrete use and often a later upgrade. Workshop is playable as described in its [survival guide](docs/WORKSHOP.md). Entries for Electrical and later ages are design targets. Names, recipes and balance remain open to playtesting.

A team earns a design once and can make replacements at the named station. A site can yield a record, test part or optional variant. Required access always has a repeatable path using capabilities the player already has. Equipment is useful when it first appears and keeps a job as more capable versions arrive. Optional combat gear and sandbox effects stay outside the main progression gates. Each vehicle or powered suit needs a service station, a visible operating cost and a recovery path when its power runs out or it is stranded.

| Insulated cable, switches and charging docks | [2.1 Local Grid](docs/PROGRESSION.md#2-electrical), after Workshop commissioning | Assemble cable and a switch using windings and common insulation such as wool. Later docks grow into vehicle and robot service points. |
| Powered scanner and repair tool | [2.1 Local Grid](docs/PROGRESSION.md#2-electrical), after the cable-fed mill proof | Build rechargeable diagnostics and repair modules from a winding, housing and cell. | Powered Assembler and Relay Module | [2.2 Production Line](docs/PROGRESSION.md#2-electrical), after a cable-fed mill job | Build the assembler from casings, windings and a drive. | Survey Terminal, Survey Mast and Survey Chart | [2.3 Survey Network](docs/PROGRESSION.md#2-electrical), after the automated batch proof | Build and cable the terminal and mast. Later depots and route beacons inherit the waypoint role. |
