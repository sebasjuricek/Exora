# Exora master design

## Player experience

Exora starts with a small Overworld workshop and grows into an automated industrial and expedition network. GregTech inspires the pacing and reuse of earlier production lines. HBM inspires large factories, hazardous facilities, and the connection between industrial power and field equipment. Exora uses original content, with fictionalized industrial and combat mechanics designed for Minecraft.

The repeatable loop is survey → process → automate → research → equip → explore. A discovery feeds the next stage of research. Earlier materials remain useful as inputs, spare parts, or infrastructure. Each age should unlock a new way to build, move, explore, or operate a base.

## Interconnected systems

- **Research and access.** A research terminal shows age milestones, branch prerequisites, and the next experiment or field discovery. Shared research unlocks production within an expedition team. Servers remain authoritative over access and permissions.
- **Manufacturing and automation.** Machine tiers add processing capabilities and throughput. Recipes reuse a small family of components so that scaling production rewards automation. Machines expose clear input, output, energy, and fault states.
- **Equipment and vehicle lifecycle.** Research provides designs. Assemblers and specialized vehicle bays manufacture frames and modules. Tool benches, suit stations, and service bays handle fitting and upgrades. Fuel or charge, condition, permissions, and replaceable parts give players reasons to return to the factory. Field repairs should let a player recover their equipment or get home.
- **Power and logistics.** Beginning with small generators, players scale through distribution, in-game nuclear power, fusion, and exotic generation. Energy powers machines and charging stations. Logistics move materials, coolant substitutes, and maintenance supplies. Fictional consumables and reactor states make complex facilities readable.
- **Encounters and exploration.** Early ruins introduce hazards through clear signals and recovery paths. Later facilities and original bosses test mobility, protection, logistics, and equipment choices. Gate encounters advance the main tree. Optional encounters offer sidegrades, more efficient production, or alternate objectives.
- **Combat and mobility.** Propeller fighters, jet fighters, helicopters, tanks, mechs, powersuits, robots, and fictional armaments serve distinct in-game roles. Their performance is balanced against Exora's own encounters. Servers govern player-versus-player behavior and terrain effects.

## Presentation and performance

Use original models, readable machine interfaces, and effects that communicate state. Include readable status cues alongside color and sound. Provide scalable visual effects. Bound ticking, pathfinding, network traffic, and automation work. Profile a running factory with several players before adding larger networks or encounters.

Finish a complete Overworld progression loop before opening orbital infrastructure, planetary expeditions, or new dimensions.
