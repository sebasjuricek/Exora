# Exora progression

One mainline age chain connects manufacturing, energy, and exploration. Branches for field equipment, power, logistics, and vehicles unlock from the current age and feed back into later milestones. The table describes planned gates and powers.

| Age | Entry gate | New capability | Optional branch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workshop | Start in an Overworld survival world | Make basic alloys and reusable mechanical parts | Improved survey and repair tools |
| Electrical | Demonstrate a powered processing cycle | Generate and distribute power and automate basic processing | Higher-throughput filters and buffers |
| Mechanized | Restore a survey station using factory-made parts | Manufacture precision components, field robots, and ground vehicles | Heavy tooling and early combat vehicles |
| Aerospace | Complete the relay recovery encounter and demonstrate a precision production line | Assemble, pilot, and service Overworld aircraft | Cargo modules and specialized airframes |
| Atomic | Recover containment research from a hazardous Overworld facility | Operate a fictional reactor facility and build long-duration powered equipment | Better instrumentation and protective modules |
| Fusion | Stabilize an in-game power network and complete a containment encounter | Sustain energy-intensive assembly, larger mechs, and advanced suits | Specialized suit mobility and factory upgrades |
| Frontier | Complete the Overworld boss chain and build an expedition platform | Stage orbital and later planetary expeditions | Further destinations and endgame sidegrades |

## Gate rules

- Gate production through clear prerequisites shown in the research interface. A typical milestone combines a manufacturing demonstration with evidence from a site or encounter.
- Give players the tools to reach and complete each mandatory gate using active and earlier age unlocks. Provide a way to retry a failed encounter or replace lost evidence.
- Share mainline research across the expedition team. Use permissions for access to team terminals, vehicles, and inventories. Let individual loadouts remain personal choices.
- Use optional encounters for alternate production routes, utilities, and equipment sidegrades. Keep mandatory unlocks out of random drop tables.
